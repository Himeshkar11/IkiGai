# 🧠 ONE VERY IMPORTANT VIBE-CODING RULE

**Don't give your AI one giant prompt saying "build my entire Life OS."**

That's how you end up with:

```text
3,000 lines
broken APIs
duplicate components
random database schemas
authentication bugs
and a beautiful UI that doesn't work.
```

Instead:

```text
PROMPT
   ↓
AI implements one phase
   ↓
YOU RUN IT
   ↓
YOU TEST IT
   ↓
FIX BUGS
   ↓
NEXT PHASE
```

The prompts above are deliberately designed around that workflow.

And your MongoDB:

```text
users
│
├── todos
│
├── foods
│
├── foodLogs
│
├── roomLogs
│
├── roomTasks
│
└── moneyTransactions
```

The **calendar is the common thread** connecting everything.

That is the design I'd strongly recommend: **one date → one snapshot of your life**.

Eventually, when you add Study, Workout, Journal, Sleep, Goals, etc., they simply become additional modules attached to that same date-based Life OS architecture.

And because you're building this for yourself rather than as a startup, resist the temptation to make it huge on day one. **A boring app that you actually open every morning is infinitely more valuable than a massive app you never finish.**
